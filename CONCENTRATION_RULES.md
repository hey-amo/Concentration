# Concentration (Memory) Game Rules

## Classic rules

1. Shuffle a set of cards containing pairs, then lay every card face down.
2. On a turn, a player turns over two cards, one at a time, so everyone can see them.
3. If the two cards form a pair, the player keeps them face up and takes another turn.
4. If they do not form a pair, leave them visible briefly, then turn both face down in
   their original places. The turn passes to the next player.
5. Continue until every pair has been found. The player with the most pairs wins.

For a solo game, the player follows the same reveal-and-match rules and tries to find
all pairs in as few turns or flips as possible.

## Rules used by this app

- A new game starts with all cards face down.
- Each selection turns up one previously hidden, unmatched card.
- After the second card is selected, both cards remain visible briefly.
- Matching cards stay face up and are marked as matched; they cannot be selected again.
- Nonmatching cards both turn face down before another selection is accepted.
- The game tracks individual card flips, awards points for a match, and uses a timer.
  These scoring and timer rules are app-specific additions, not requirements of the
  classic game.
